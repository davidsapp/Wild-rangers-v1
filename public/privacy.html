Wild Rangers Adventure

Privacy Policy for Parents and Guardians

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Wild Rangers Adventure is an educational safari game designed for children aged 6–8. We aim to help children explore wildlife and learn about nature while minimising the personal information they need to provide.

1. Information children are asked to provide

The game is designed to be played without requiring a child's real name, email address, telephone number, home address, photograph, or personal account. Children can choose a ranger outfit, complete missions, answer quizzes, and earn badges without identifying themselves.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves certain progress and preferences in the browser's local storage, including completed missions, stars, the best quiz score, the selected outfit, the chosen language, and the parent PIN.

This information is intended to remain in the browser on the device being used. Progress is not automatically synchronised between devices. Clearing site data or changing browsers may remove or make saved information unavailable.

3. Parent PIN and controls

The parent area provides access to gameplay progress, PIN changes, and a progress-reset option. The PIN is a basic access barrier, not strong authentication. It should not be reused from sensitive accounts.

The current reset option clears completed missions and stars but may not clear every saved preference or quiz score. Parents can clear this site's browser data to remove locally stored game information, although this also removes saved settings and progress.

4. Hosting and technical information

The game is hosted as a static site by Render and is available at wild-rangers-v1.onrender.com . The hosting provider and other network infrastructure may process technical connection information as needed to deliver and protect the website.

Render states that static sites do not emit Render service logs. This does not establish that no technical information is processed elsewhere in the delivery infrastructure. We have not independently verified every infrastructure-level data practice.

The game code reviewed to date does not show an intentional collection of children's names or email addresses, targeted advertising, or an external analytics service. This statement is limited to the code reviewed and is not a guarantee about every service involved in hosting the website.

5. Accounts, advertising, and payments

The current game does not require a child account. Its premium screen is a preview of possible future subscriptions; it does not currently process payments.

If accounts, payments, advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are introduced, we will review their privacy implications and update this policy as appropriate before enabling them.

6. Sharing and security

We do not intentionally sell children's personal information or use the reviewed game code to send children's gameplay progress to advertisers. No online service or local storage system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Parents' questions and requests

Parents and legal guardians can contact us about privacy, technical support, game progress, and future subscription enquiries.

Email: wildrangers.support@gmail.com

Please do not email us a child's sensitive personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game's features or data practices change. Parents should review this page periodically, particularly before using future paid features.
